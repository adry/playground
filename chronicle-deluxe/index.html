How the house works

From a problem we know to a business that pays its way.

Every venture moves through the same four stages. Some take a weekend. Some take several seasons.

  1. 01

    Identify

    We start with problems we have lived. Nineteen years inside fashion operations is a long list of things that should work better, and a clear view of why they don't.

  2. 02

    Build

    A small team with the right tools. AI lets us go from idea to App Store in a weekend and from pilot to product in a season. We validate with real customers before we commit.

  3. 03

    Operate

    Every venture has to stand on its own margins. We run what we build, from customer support to the wholesale season to the integrations that keep it all connected.

  4. 04

    Compound

    What one venture learns, the next one inherits. Code, playbooks, suppliers, customers and judgement flow between them, so each new business starts further ahead.

Principles

Five principles we operate by.

  1. 01

    Margins over moonshots.

    Every venture has to pay its own way. Growth matters; gross margin matters more.

  2. 02

    Operators, not observers.

    We have run the warehouse, the PLM rollout and the wholesale season. We build from the floor up.

  3. 03

    Ship, then refine.

    A working product in a customer's hands teaches more than a quarter of planning.

  4. 04

    Taste is a feature.

    The back office deserves good design too: the invoice, the onboarding, the error message.

  5. 05

    Compound across ventures.

    Code, playbooks and lessons move freely between businesses. Each one makes the next one faster.

Founder

Built by an operator. Run like one.

Chronicle Deluxe is led by Adrien Forray, who spent nineteen years inside fashion operations, from luxury houses including LVMH, Kering and Burberry to Scandinavia's contemporary brands. Raw materials, sourcing, supply chain, and ERP and PLM rollouts.

In 2026 he shipped an iOS app in a weekend, more than two decades after failing his first programming course. Chronicle Deluxe exists in that gap: deep industry knowledge, now paired with tools that let a small team build what it once could only specify.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

What is Chronicle Deluxe?

A multidisciplinary business incubator. We build and run our own companies in fashion, AI, software and iOS apps, and we take on transformation and AI projects for other businesses.

Can I hire Chronicle Deluxe?

Yes. The fashion transformation consultancy helps brands improve operations, supply chain and systems such as ERP and PLM. The AI implementation practice builds agents, automations and internal tools that go into production and stay there.

What kind of businesses do you incubate?

Businesses that solve a problem we have lived from the inside, that a small team with the right tools can build quickly, and that can stand on their own margins. Today that spans PLM software, B2B wholesale, menswear and iOS apps.

How do I pitch an idea or propose a partnership?

Email hello@chronicledeluxe.com with the problem, who has it, and why now. Every message is read.
