Chronicle Deluxe: One house. Many businesses

Chronicle Deluxe is a multidisciplinary incubator. Since 2007 we have helped fashion and luxury brands run better, alongside key industry actors and academic partners. Today we also build and run our own businesses. Get in touch

Scroll Seven chapters · One page

We are AI first: supporting brands with AI implementation and strategy across design, product development, buying, sales and operations. We also build a few of our own: a Scandinavian label, iPhone apps, a wholesale platform. Outside fashion, every one of them started as a fix for our own daily problems.

What we do, and the systems we deploy to do it.
